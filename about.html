Our Story & Philosophy

The Story of North Star Bakery

North Star Bakery began in 2014 as a humble farmers' market stall founded by head baker Rick James. Driven by a desire to bring real, nutrient-dense bread back to the community table, Rick restored a wood-fired deck oven and began experimenting with heirloom wheat varieties.

Today, our bakery serves hundreds of daily regulars while remaining steadfastly committed to slow fermentation, small-batch baking, and artisanal craftsmanship.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

Great bread begins long before dough reaches the oven. We work directly with regional organic farmers to source grain that is grown sustainably without synthetic pesticides.

  • Freshly Milled Whole Grains: We stone-mill select heritage grains on-site weekly to preserve natural oils and vitamins.
  • Pure Ingredients: We use filtered water, unrefined sea salt, cultured European butter, and zero commercial yeast additives.
  • Sustainability Focus: 100% of our daily surplus bread is donated to local food banks or composted with partner community gardens.
